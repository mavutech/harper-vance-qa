# Legal and tax approval record

## Decision

The platform owner confirmed on October 12, 2026 that qualified legal counsel
approved the current Harper Vance customer-facing legal package and agreement
process, and that a qualified tax adviser approved the current Stripe product
tax treatment.

## Scope

This approval covers the October 12, 2026 Terms of Service and Privacy Policy,
the current customer agreement and order workflow, the current business-to-
business license offers, and the current Stripe tax treatment for those offers.

It does not authorize a switch from Stripe Test to Live, a real transaction, or
changes to the approved documents or tax model.

## Evidence handling

Privileged advice, adviser identities, and source documents remain in the
platform owner's approved external records. They are intentionally not stored
in source control. This repository records the decision, date, scope, and
operational effect only.

## Renewal triggers

Obtain and record a new approval before using a materially changed legal
document, contracting flow, product offer, billing model, customer geography,
or tax treatment.
