# Harper Vance Public Messaging Specification

## Audience

The page is written for professional NQ trading organizations, especially proprietary desks, funds, research leaders, and risk or oversight teams. These buyers need to understand what they receive, how they can evaluate it, and how it fits into an existing organization. They are more likely to trust a precise record than a promotional performance claim.

## StoryBrand flow

| Story role | Harper Vance message |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | A professional desk that must evaluate target intelligence responsibly. |
| Problem | A target cannot be judged when its original issue details, context, and outcome are scattered or rewritten after the fact. |
| Guide | Harper Vance preserves the target and the record around it. |
| Plan | Review the target during the session, inspect the completed daily record, then evaluate weekly and historical behavior. |
| Call to action | Request the latest completed-session report. |
| Success | Trading, research, and oversight can evaluate the same evidence over time. |
| Failure avoided | The organization does not have to rely on isolated claims or incomplete recaps. |

## Message hierarchy

### Hero

- Eyebrow: `NQ market intelligence`
- Headline: `NQ target intelligence your desk can evaluate over time.`
- Description: `Harper Vance publishes qualifying intraday NQ targets and preserves the complete record—current targets, daily outcomes, weekly behavior, and historical comparisons—so trading, research, and oversight can evaluate the intelligence over time.`
- Primary action: `Request the latest report`
- Secondary action: `View licensing`

The hero must answer three questions quickly: what the product is, what the client receives, and why the retained record matters.

### Product sequence

| Stage | Title | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | During the session | Qualifying NQ targets are published with direction, timestamp, and session context through approved delivery channels. |
| 2 | After the session | Every target is retained with its recorded outcome and included in the completed-session email report. |
| 3 | Over time | Weekly summaries and historical views help the team evaluate behavior across completed sessions. |

### Dashboard

- Section title: `Review the intelligence from one controlled system of record.`
- Introduction: `Authorized users can review current targets, completed-session analysis, weekly summaries, and historical behavior without changing the desk's execution workflow.`

| View | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Today's Targets | Review current and unresolved targets with their original issue details, live session status, and recorded outcomes. |
| Daily Performance Record | Examine the complete daily target log, session replay, timing, retraction, and session-period behavior. |
| Weekly Summary | Review week-level outcomes, timing, retraction patterns, daily variation, and sample-aware comparisons. |
| Historical & Rolling | Compare rolling trends, directional behavior, and day-by-day records across retained history. |

Supporting statement:

> Partial periods, small samples, unresolved targets, and unavailable aggregates remain visible so teams can interpret the record with its limitations intact.

### Licensing

The pricing section must make the shared product clear before describing tier differences.

Shared across licenses:

- Qualifying intraday NQ target intelligence
- Secure authenticated dashboard
- Today's Targets and Daily Performance Record
- Weekly Summary
- Historical & Rolling analysis
- Completed-session daily and weekly reports
- Original timestamps, context, status, and outcomes
- Email and Slack or Microsoft Teams delivery

Tier differences should remain limited to users, retained history, delivery methods and integrations, internal usage rights, and service commitments. The page should say that dashboard views are shared across licenses while history, users, integrations, usage rights, and service commitments vary by tier.

## Approved vocabulary

- Target intelligence
- Qualifying target
- Recorded outcome
- Current and unresolved targets
- Completed-session record
- Daily Performance Record
- Weekly Summary
- Historical and rolling analysis
- Sample-aware comparison
- Secure authenticated dashboard
- Decision-support intelligence

## Claims to avoid

- Signal service, alerts product, or winning trades
- Trade recommendations or execution instructions
- Real-time, low-latency, or execution-ready
- Guarantees, promised accuracy, or projected returns
- The internal chart interval
- Immutable record
- Exact performance percentages on the public page
- Dashboard screenshots until a clean, approved, non-sensitive image is available
- Any claim that a license tier unlocks a dashboard view unless that policy is implemented and verified

## Conversion principle

The page should not attempt to prove performance publicly. Its job is to make the product concrete, show the evaluation path, establish institutional discipline, and move a qualified visitor to request the latest completed-session report.
