# Sales clarity: customer story

> Historical record. Current scope and acceptance gates: [current-sales-plan.md](current-sales-plan.md). Earlier headlines, CTA choices and completion claims below are not current instructions.

Phase 2, October 19, 2026. Commercial boundaries: `sales-clarity-offer.md`.

## Summary

Help a professional NQ trading or research leader understand the observations,
inspect the record, and decide whether the service fits the desk's workflow.
The customer is making a research-adoption decision; returns are not promised.

## Key points

- Hero: **Evaluate NQ target research with the original record in view.**
- Explain what is published, what happened afterward, and how a desk can review it.
- Primary working action: **Request a Sample Report**, using the existing
  verified report request. Do not relabel this as a walkthrough booking.
- Secondary action: **Explore the Report Format**, opening an immediate example.
- Keep **Request Access** on pricing cards and Client Login in navigation.
- A walkthrough action requires a confirmed destination and responsible owner.

## Page sequence

1. Hero and two clear actions.
2. Buyer problem with a representative target record.
3. Target definition and the during-session, after-session, over-time deliverables.
4. Report format, buyer fit, and three-step evaluation path.
5. Dashboard views explained through concrete review questions.
6. Methodology and limitations.
7. Licenses with unchanged prices and confirmed limits.
8. FAQ and company/legal identity.
9. Report request with verification and review expectations before submission.

## Risks with mitigations

- Never describe invented data as real or historical performance.
- Source timestamps are not proof of customer receipt or exact publication.
- Outcomes concern Harper Vance targets, not the client's trades.
- Replace retraction with price movement away from a target; replace human
  consumption with dashboard and email review.
- Remove internal certification language and unsupported response deadlines.
- Do not promise time saved, returns, independent audits, or guarantees.

## Recommendations

Keep implementation copy in the shared page language file, disclosures near
examples, and definitions in a keyboard-accessible FAQ. Preserve the restrained
visual system. Actual screenshots and reports require publication clearance.

## Next steps

Prepare evidence, implement the sequence, and test navigation and request
behavior. Buyer feedback and production measurement remain future validation.
