# Sales feedback and measurement playbook

> Historical record. Current scope and acceptance gates: [current-sales-plan.md](current-sales-plan.md). Earlier headlines, CTA choices and completion claims below are not current instructions.

Phase 6 preparation, October 19, 2026. No buyer interviews or post-release
measurement have occurred. This is the operating handoff, not a results report.

## Summary

Judge the page by whether relevant NQ teams understand the offer and progress
to an informed conversation. Page clicks alone do not establish sales success.

## Key points

- Owner: founder/commercial operator. Technical owner maintains event integrity.
- Start the observation window when the revised page is actually published.
- Use three to five qualified buyers for initial comprehension feedback.
- Review approximately 30 days of outcomes, extending the window when the
  qualified sample is too small. No automatic monitor or outreach is scheduled.
- Keep personal prospect information in the approved CRM, outside source control.
- Keep analytics disabled unless its existing enablement and applicable consent
  requirements have been satisfied. Use manual aggregate counts if disabled.

For every rate, retain numerator, denominator and time window. Do not divide
meetings from one cohort by requests from another. Do not classify anonymous
visitors as qualified buyers. Report raw counts when attribution is incomplete.
Do not infer confirmed meetings, delivered reports or purchases from button clicks.

## Buyer interview script

Show the page without first explaining the product. Ask:

1. In your own words, what does Harper Vance provide?
2. What would your team use it for, if anything?
3. What does a reached target mean? Does this describe your trading return?
4. What would you inspect before paying for it?
5. What do you expect to happen after the primary button?
6. Which license appears relevant, and what remains unclear?
7. What would stop you from requesting a report or continuing a discussion?

Record themes and anonymized observations in the review summary. Do not
invent responses or store names, emails or company-sensitive details in Git.

## Risks with mitigations

- Low volume: extend observation and use interviews; do not claim statistical lift.
- More unqualified leads: review buyer fit before optimizing raw submission counts.
- Repeated objections: revise the relevant explanation, not every section at once.
- Misleading evidence: retain the same selection and labeling rules for sample reports.
- Premature discounts: first distinguish unclear value from genuine budget mismatch.

## Recommendations

Keep the initial release as a baseline. Review recurring misunderstandings before
changing pricing or starting an experiment. Use a simple walkthrough agenda:
inspect one session, explain outcome limits, review dashboard questions, confirm
history/delivery/user requirements, and agree a next step or a clear no-fit decision.
