## Risks with mitigations

- Historical status becoming a new blocker: reconcile dated records before reopening an old issue.
- Unsupported claims: distinguish an implemented path from deployed configuration and confirmed receipt.
- Data exposure: commit reusable instructions only; exclude account identifiers, credentials, private receiver URLs, prospect information, and detailed licensed exports.
- Scope expansion: document product gaps without implementing them.

## Recommendations

Lead with a reviewable workflow: follow a target, inspect its context, review the outcome, and evaluate the complete session. Do not lead with a selected accuracy percentage or unverified competitor comparison.
