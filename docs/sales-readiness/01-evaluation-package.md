## Risks with mitigations

Keep earlier-calculation warnings next to summary figures. Do not turn complete available records into a claim of independently complete market history. Do not average daily percentages to calculate a monthly reached rate. Treat unavailable measurements as unavailable, not zero.

## Recommendations

Use the brief for owner review first. For an authorized prospect, offer the full-period record with the explanation, not a standalone accuracy graphic. Preserve the original sample-report and video dates when demonstrating those separate artifacts.
